What Oscar is built on

The body is chillibasket's open-source Wall-E replica: about 310 printed parts, two drive motors, seven servos, an Arduino and a Raspberry Pi. The stock build is remote controlled. We add three AI layers: Oscar follows faces, talks back through a language model, and learns her toys from examples she shows him.

This is an unofficial fan build and a personal project. WALL-E is a character owned by Disney and Pixar. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by either company.

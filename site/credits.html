Credits, licence, privacy

Credits

Whose work this builds on

  • chillibasket designed the robot and published the CAD files, the assembly instructions and the Arduino and web interface code. This project would not exist without that work.
  • The remixes Oscar uses are by JaZzMaN251, ZDC, dmdiego, Guenter, Fendermann and rene1960. Each one is linked with its licence on the design changes page.
  • WALL-E-DORA by mneuhaus is a more advanced build in the same lineage. The rear battery flap and the front power button are his ideas.
  • Microduck and Open Duck Mini are the projects to follow for robots that learn to walk in simulation.
  • The look follows the Grounded Precision design system, with two colours borrowed from the build: Sunflower Yellow from Oscar's body and Hot Pink from his name plate. The typeface is Geist by Vercel, under the SIL Open Font License, served from this site.

Licence

Our text, our pictures and our own CAD files are under the MIT licence.

That covers only what we made from scratch. A part changed from chillibasket's files, from a remix or from another builder's model keeps the licence of the original, and is marked with it where it is published. Code derived from chillibasket's code is published under GPL-3.0, like the original.

Privacy

My daughter builds Oscar with me. This site does not give her name or age, and it shows no photo, video or recording of her voice.

Photos show Oscar and his parts. Location data is removed from every photo before it is published.

The site sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. Links to other sites only load them when you click.

Pages are delivered through Cloudflare, which handles visitors' IP addresses to do that. Cloudflare adds no scripts or analytics to the pages.

This is an unofficial fan build and a personal project. WALL-E is a character owned by Disney and Pixar. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by either company.
